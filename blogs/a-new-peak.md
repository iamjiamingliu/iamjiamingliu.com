I thought I peaked in high school.
In hindsight, it definitely was a quite a local maxima marked by insane vision, execution, and dedication.

I reached a new peak.
Between November 2026 to January 2027,
I received 5 summer internship interviews:
TikTok, Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, and Citadel.
Out of these companies, Microsoft, despite I aced the interview and the intervewer said "you made a strong impression on me", rejected me.
But thankfully, I scored every other hit.
I was offered to join TikTok search autocomplete,
Google Ads Personalization Team,
Google Core (homomorphic encryption team),
OpenAI ChatGPT Ads,
Citadel Hedge Fund Equities Risk Engineering Team,
and Citadel Securities Credit Trading Team.

Literally, all the big names, I scored them. On good teams too.
A LinkedIn post for these would go insane.

And to spice up the shit further,
on March,
I persuaded Manu Bhat to apply to YCombinator with me,
and we got in.
They had to defer the decision to later,
but in effect,
we got in.

What in the fuck. The holy trinity of CS undergrads are:

1. AI Firms: OpenAI, Anthropic, Deepmind
2. Quant: Citadel, Jane Street, HRT
3. Startup: YCombinator

Plus the old big name of Google.

And I scored them all.
Legitimately, I might be the only person in 2026 being able to score them all.
What. The. Fuck.

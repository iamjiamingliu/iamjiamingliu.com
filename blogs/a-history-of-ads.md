Well, I'm about to start my summer internship at ChatGPT ads.
I'm super grateful for this opportunity,
and I certainly don't want to walk in the door knowing nothing about ads.

Ads are something we see everywhere,
but we rarely give further thoughts about the business intuition, domain mechanisms, and engineering behind ads.
We just take it for granted that ads exist everywhere,
as banners that encroach every website's screen or those unskippable 15-seconds Youtube ads.

But I'm sure that the internet didn't start this way full of ads.
I think the internet started clean of ads, and somehow along the way,
it just grew to the barbaric extent it is everywhere today.

Thus, to understand ads, I need to understand how it got from nowhere to what it is today.
Hence, the history of ads.
